[[curie.ged#@S2@|Parish registers of Warsaw]], held by
[[curie.ged#@R2@|the State Archive in Warsaw]], carry the births of
[[curie.ged#@I3@|Władysław Skłodowski]] and [[curie.ged#@I4@|Bronisława Boguska]].

Nothing in this vault has been checked against the originals; the dates come from
published biographies, and the citation says where they would be confirmed.
