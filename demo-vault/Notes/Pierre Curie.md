Physicist, born in Paris in 1859, killed by a horse-drawn cart on the rue
Dauphine in 1906. His record is [[curie.ged#@I2@|Pierre Curie]].

![[Media/pierre-curie.svg]]

He worked with his brother [[curie.ged#@I11@|Jacques Curie]] on piezoelectricity
before the work on [[Radium]] with [[curie.ged#@I1@|Marie Skłodowska-Curie]].
