Physicist and chemist, born in Warsaw in 1867, died at Sancellemoz in 1934.
Her record is [[curie.ged#@I1@|Marie Skłodowska-Curie]], and the two prizes are
recorded as a shared note: [[curie.ged#@N1@|Two Nobel Prizes]].

![[Media/marie-sklodowska-curie.svg]]

She married [[curie.ged#@I2@|Pierre Curie]] in Sceaux in 1895. Their work on
[[Radium]] is the subject of the 1903 prize, [[curie.ged#@S1@|the source]] for
both of their records.

Her parents were teachers in Warsaw — [[curie.ged#@I3@|Władysław Skłodowski]]
taught mathematics and physics, [[curie.ged#@I4@|Bronisława Boguska]] ran a
boarding school — and she was the youngest of five children.
