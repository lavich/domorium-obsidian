Isolated from pitchblende at the École de physique et de chimie in Paris,
announced in December 1898 by [[curie.ged#@I1@|Marie Skłodowska-Curie]] and
[[curie.ged#@I2@|Pierre Curie]].

The work is cited in both of their records through
[[curie.ged#@S1@|The Nobel Prize in Physics 1903]], and again in hers through
[[curie.ged#@S3@|the 1911 prize in chemistry]], which was awarded for the
isolation of the metal itself.

See also [[Nobel Prizes in the family]].
