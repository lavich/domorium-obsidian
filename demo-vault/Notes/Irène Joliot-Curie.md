for the discovery of artificial radioactivity — the second Nobel Prize in the
family, and not the last: see [[Nobel Prizes in the family]].

Their children, [[curie.ged#@I16@|Hélène Langevin-Joliot]] and
[[curie.ged#@I17@|Pierre Joliot]], are living, so their records carry a year of
birth and nothing more.
