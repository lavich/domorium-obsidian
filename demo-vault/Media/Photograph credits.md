# Photograph credits

Every photograph in this folder is in the public domain and came from
Wikimedia Commons. They are here so the plugin has something real to show:
faces rather than grey rectangles, and group photographs whose `CROP`
rectangles frame an actual person.

| File | What it shows | Photographer | Date |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `marie-sklodowska-curie.jpg` | Marie Skłodowska-Curie | Henri Manuel | about 1920 |
| `pierre-curie.jpg` | Pierre Curie | Dujardin | about 1906 |
| `curie-family-1908.jpg` | Pierre, Irène and Marie in the garden of the house on Boulevard Kellermann | unknown | before 1908 |
| `joliot-curie-couple.jpg` | Frédéric and Irène Joliot-Curie, as a pair of portraits in one frame | Harcourt | 1930s |

Each was downloaded from Wikimedia Commons, which records them as public
domain, and taken at a reduced size rather than at the original resolution.
The originals and their licence statements are under the same names at
[commons.wikimedia.org](https://commons.wikimedia.org).

## Why two of them are cropped

`curie-family-1908.jpg` holds three people and `joliot-curie-couple.jpg`
holds two. `curie.ged` names a rectangle within each, which is how GEDCOM
says *this person is the one on the left*. The rectangles were measured
against these files and will not survive replacing a file with a differently
sized one.

`lost-daguerreotype.jpg` is named by `curie.ged` and is deliberately absent:
it is what the plugin shows for a file the vault does not hold.
