How to read the mockup

  1. The file opens as genealogy. “Source” in the header is the same file in today's editor, on the lines of the selected fact or record.
  2. Nothing is written to the file. Corrections happen in the source editor only: every fact and record has “Show in source”.
  3. The right sidebar shows what the selection rests on. With nothing selected, the whole record. A fact shows its own sources, and below them, apart, the sources, notes and media of the whole record.
  4. Gaps: “no source” means the fact cites nothing itself; a dot in the index and the tree means the person has no source at all, neither on the record nor on any fact.
  5. Vault notes are shown with the sentence around their link to the record. The mockup does not compare a note's text with the facts; the reader spots disagreements.
  6. Tree: two generations up and down, siblings collapsed, ▲ and ▼ mark relatives beyond the edge. A click moves the focus.
  7. Timeline: the person's own events and those of close relatives (parents, siblings, spouses, children, grandchildren) up to the end of the person's life.
