What to try

  • Drag to move, wheel to zoom, Fit to see everyone.
  • Hover a person: their parents, spouses and children and the lines to them light up.
  • Hover a person and choose Branch: only their ancestors and descendants stay, with their spouses.
  • Hover a family node for the marriage; click a person: in the plugin that opens their page.

How it is laid out

  • Generations top to bottom, in the same order as the timeline of the file: siblings together, a spouse beside the one they married.
  • Spouses form one block, the husband on the left. Someone married twice stands between their two spouses, so one of the two pairs reads the other way.
  • A line meets a block where it belongs: a family between its spouses, a child at their own card. So the husband's parents and siblings settle on the left of a couple and the wife's on the right.
  • Blocks are reordered generation by generation to cross as few lines as possible, then pulled towards their parents and children; the subtitle says how many crossings are left.
  • Each family is a node between the spouses; a line to a child several generations down keeps a narrow lane free in the rows it passes.
