What to try

  • Hover a person: their row, their parents and children light up, everyone else fades.
  • Hover a point for its date as the file writes it, and its place. Click it: in the plugin that is Show in source.
  • Click a name: in the plugin that opens the person's page.
  • Zoom, scroll sideways; names and the year axis stay in place.
