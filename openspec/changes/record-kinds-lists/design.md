## Context

This plan was first written against `main` and is now built in branch `lavich/record-fields-tree`, on top of the change of that name, and archived after it. What the branch's code does today, as far as this change touches it:

- The list (`src/desk/listRows.ts`, `src/desk/fileList.ts`) has four kinds, `people`, `families`, `sources`, `places`: `ListKind`, `LIST_KINDS`, `COLUMNS`, `cellsOf`, `sortValue`, `listRows`, the label tables of `fileList.ts`, `countLabel`. `recordKind(index, xref)` answers a kind for a person, a family, a source and a place, and `listSelection` passes on only what `recordKind` knows. `ListBoard.place()` keeps the Places against a selection of another kind that did not come from the card (`this.kind === "places" && kind !== "places" && !fromCard`); `activate()` selects `row.xref ?? placeId(key)`. People, families and sources are searched by lower case only; places through `foldText`.
- `resized()` decides the compact layout from the root's width with `COMPACT_BELOW` (640) and `WIDE_FROM` (680), from a `ResizeObserver` on the root and on a probe row; nothing measures the kind buttons, which simply shrink.
- The read model (`src/genealogy/build.ts`) walks the top-level symbols once per revision; it keeps `declared` (every symbol with an identifier) for `record(xref)`, `objects` for `media(xref)`, and `repositories`, which nothing reads since `record-fields-tree` removed `Source.heldAt`. `collectMediaLinks` reads a record's own `OBJE` lines (level 1) only. `recordLines` gives a root a text only for `NOTE` and `SNOTE`.
- `pageKind` (`src/desk/deskState.ts`) answers `person`, `family`, `source`, `media`, `place`; `PageKind` has no `event` any more. It decides what a link selects (`routeLink`, `GedcomView.goToXref`, `goToRecord`), what the cursor selects (`GedcomView.has()` into `shouldFollowCursor`, `TabModes.setMode` on leaving Source), what `TabModes.select` accepts and keeps (`textChanged`), and what `renderPointer` draws as a link. `shownRange` reads people, families, sources and media.
- The card (`src/desk/recordCard.ts`) has no header bar and no history. `drawRecord` builds the `PageHost`, works out `writes` from `tab.version()` and `tab.describes(opening)`, sets `uneditable` where the tab writes but this record cannot, and draws person, family and source through `drawTree` (`personPage.ts`), media and place through their own pages. `hasTree` is person, family or source. The announcement uses a private `recordName`; `mediaPage.ts` has another; `lineView.ts` has `recordTitle`, which the tree uses for pointers and the root's summary. The empty state is `card.empty` when the file has no people, families or sources.
- `renderPointer` (`src/desk/detail/lineView.ts`) draws a pointer to a shared note as the note's text and lines and returns; any other pointer is a link where `pageKind` answers and plain text otherwise. So a `REPO` line in a source's tree is plain text today, and becomes a link with no change of its own once `pageKind` answers for a repository (checked in the code; the tree calls `renderPointer` for every pointer row, `tree.ts` `paintValue`).
- `GedcomView.selectRecord` places the cursor in Source at `records().find(identifier === xref).start`, which the header, having no identifier, never matches. `goToRecord` selects only a record with an identifier that `pageKind` knows.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** every record a file declares, except its trailer and records without an identifier, can be found in the list and opened on a card that is its tree; adding a kind follows the pattern the places set.

**Non-Goals:** a tree for media; selecting the new records by the cursor; marking them in the graph or the timeline.

## Decisions

**The kinds and their rows.** Eight kinds: `people`, `families`, `sources`, `places`, `media`, `notes`, `repositories`, `other`. A record belongs to exactly one. `other` lists the header first, under the synthetic id `header:` (`HEADER_ID` in `deskState.ts`, as a place has `place:`), then, in file order, every top-level record with an identifier whose tag no other kind takes: `SUBM`, 5.5.1 `SUBN`, a custom tag, and any record of the standard no kind lists. `TRLR` and records without an identifier are not listed (M4). The header's row carries `header:` as its `xref`, so `keyOf`, `markedKey`, `activate` and `place` treat it as any record.

**Columns.** Media: Title, File, Format, Linked from. Notes: Text, Linked from. Repositories: Name, Address, Sources. Other: Kind, Name, Linked from. New `SortKey`s: `text`, `file`, `format`, `address`, `kind`, `linkedFrom`; `title`, `name` and `sources` are reused, `sortValue` dispatching by kind as now. Under any sort the header row stays first and the sort orders the rest (M4).

**Linked from counts records, not lines (S5).** It is the number of distinct records — the header among them — with at least one line, at any depth, whose payload is a pointer to the record; a record pointing at itself, `@VOID@` and a `CONT` or `CONC` line do not count. Records, because the Sources' *Cited by* already counts records (`citingRecords` takes a `Set`), so the two numbers of one list mean the same thing, and because a person who cites a note twice is still one place to look. The repositories' **Sources** is the same count restricted to source records.

**The media count differs from the media card, and says why.** The media card's *Used by* lists `media.links`, which `collectMediaLinks` reads from a record's own `OBJE` lines: where a record shows the medium as its own picture, with a crop and a caption. *Linked from* also counts a record that points at the medium beneath an event or a citation. In `curie.ged` the two agree (`@O5@`: Marie and Pierre, 2). Making them agree everywhere means changing the card's list, which the user kept as it is; recorded under Open Questions.

**The counts are a new deep walk, lazy, once per index (M1).** The existing pass reads only the top-level symbols and, for citations and media, a few known tags; no pass today sees every pointer. `build.ts` keeps the top-level symbols in its closure and, on the first `linkedFrom` or `sourcesOf` call, walks every line of every record once into a `Map<string, Set<string>>` (target → pointing records), kept for the index. Lazy rather than eager, because a reader who never opens these kinds or their columns pays nothing, and the walk is one per revision either way; the people of a 20 000-person file are not delayed. Which records each kind holds is read eagerly in the existing top-level loop (a symbol per record, no conversion); a row's texts are converted on first request and cached, as `record(xref)` already is.

**One naming function (S3).** `recordTitle(index, xref)` in `lineView.ts` becomes the only one: the list's first text column (Title of media, Text of notes, Name of repositories and other records), the card's announcement (replacing `recordName` in `recordCard.ts`), pointer link text (already), the tree root's closed summary (already) and the media card's *Used by* (replacing `recordName` in `mediaPage.ts`). It adds: a place by name; a medium by title, else the base name of its first file; a note by the first non-empty line of its joined text — the `RecordLine` text, which `recordLines` builds from the label and the `CONC`/`CONT` lines, not the symbol's label (M1); a repository and a submitter by `NAME`; any other record by `NAME`, `TITL`, the first child value, else its kind as the tree labels it; `header:` as *File header* (`record.header`).

**Search (M5).** Each new kind searches what its row shows, the note its whole joined text, the other records their kind both in the reader's words and as the tag is written. The four new kinds fold through `foldText`, as the places do: notes are free prose in any language and a repository's address is a place. The people, families and sources keep their case-only match; changing them is not this change's (Non-goals). Strings: `list.search.media` "Title, file, format…", `list.search.notes` "Text of the note…", `list.search.repositories` "Name, address…", `list.search.other` "Kind, name…", and `list.nothing.*` saying the same.

**Counts are digits (M2).** `fill` in `src/i18n/index.ts` writes `String(count)`; the existing counts read `17 people` and a large file `20000 people`. The scenarios read `1478 notes`; grouping digits by locale is a change for every kind, not this one.

**Eight kinds, and a dropdown measured, not a breakpoint (S4).** `fileList.ts` keeps, beside the visible bar, a hidden probe of the eight buttons (`aria-hidden`, out of the flow, the same classes), so their natural width is measurable while the dropdown is shown. `resized()`, already called by the `ResizeObserver` on the root, reads the probe's width and the search icon's, and switches to the dropdown when the bar is narrower than their sum, back to buttons only when it is wider than the sum by `KINDS_MARGIN` (40 px, the gap `COMPACT_BELOW`/`WIDE_FROM` already use), so a width at the edge does not flap. The probe is measured again on every resize, which also covers a change of language or font size. The dropdown is a `select.dropdown` named by `list.kind` ("Kind of records"), placed where the buttons were in the tab order, 44 px on touch through the rules the sort select already has. Alternative: a container query. Rejected by the user: the width the buttons need depends on the language and the font.

**Keeping the kind against a person from outside (B1).** `place()` generalises the Places rule: while the list shows `places`, `media`, `notes`, `repositories` or `other`, a person, a family or a source selected from outside (`!fromCard`) marks nothing and does not switch the kind; a record of any of those five kinds switches to its own kind and is brought into view, from anywhere; a record pressed in the card switches as today. `recordKind` and `listSelection` answer the new kinds (S1), so a selected note, repository or other record stays marked and is brought into view.

**One page kind for the new cards: `record`.** `pageKind` answers `record` for a shared note, a repository, a submitter, a submission, a record of the file's own and `header:`. `followsCursor(kind)` is true for `person`, `family`, `source`, `media` only, and `GedcomView.has()`, `cursorSettled` and `TabModes.setMode` ask it instead of `pageKind`. The cursor in Source thus keeps selecting what it selects today; note that today that includes a multimedia record, which the user described as "people, families and sources (as today)"; the plan keeps today's behaviour and flags it.

**The new cards are trees (`src/desk/recordPage.ts`).** `renderRecordPage` calls `drawTree` with no facts, as `renderSourcePage` does; `hasTree` includes `record`, so these cards take the tree's header bar, a slot of the session's twenty and a remembered scroll. The root is labelled by `tagLabel` of the opening tag; `record.tag.HEAD` (*Header*) and `record.tag.SUBN` (*Submission*) are added to both catalogues (M4). `recordLines` gives a root a text wherever its opening line has a payload that is not a pointer (a custom record's value), not only for notes, and the tree shows the root's value: a note's text as a note (folded, an editable prose field where the card writes), anything else as a field.

**The header is read-only; every other new card edits.** In `drawRecord`, `writes` is false for `header:` and `uneditable` is false too, so the tree is drawn as a plain read-only tree without the "cannot be edited here" line, which would wrongly suggest a missing dialect. Changing `GEDC.VERS`, `CHAR` or the schema from a field rewrites how the whole file is read, which the card cannot show safely. A submitter and the other records edit within what `describeStructure` describes, which is the existing rule for every tree; a custom record is described with `extension: true` and keeps its fields and removals and no menu, as a custom line does.

***Open note* (B2).** `renderPointer` keeps drawing a shared note's text and lines inline and adds, after the text, a small `clickable-icon` button named `desk.openNote` with a focus key `t:<node key>:open`, that calls `host.openRecord(xref)`. It is a card-origin selection, so it does not expand the sidebar and switches the list to the Notes. A pointer to a repository or a submitter is a link through `renderPointer` once `pageKind` answers `record`, with no other change.

**Source mode (S2).** `shownRange` reads `index.record(xref)?.range` for `record` kinds and the header's range for `header:` (`index.record(HEADER_ID)`), so switching to Source puts the cursor at the selected note, repository, submitter, other record or header. `GedcomView.selectRecord` places the cursor from `shownRange(...).start` instead of looking the identifier up in `records()`, so a link or *Go to record* to the header in Source puts the cursor on `0 HEAD`.

***Go to record* on the header opens its card (M8).** A `goToTarget(record)` in `deskState.ts` answers `HEADER_ID` for a `GedcomRecord` whose tag is `HEAD` and the identifier otherwise; `goToRecord` selects it where `pageKind` answers, and the trailer and records without an identifier still switch to Source. Copying a link is offered for any selected record with an identifier; the header and a place offer none, as `identifiedRecord` already decides.

**Empty state (M7).** `card.empty` is shown when the file declares no record with an identifier at all; a file of notes alone says to select a record. The header alone does not count, so a file with nothing but `HEAD` and `TRLR` still says it holds nothing to show.

**Phone and compact rows (S6).** Two lines per row, as the existing kinds: media — title and count / file · format; notes — text over both lines (`-webkit-line-clamp: 2`), count at the end of the first; repositories — name and count / address; other — name and count / kind. `countLabel` gains `linkedFrom` (`list.compact.linkedFrom`, "Linked from {count}", as `citedBy` reads) and reuses `list.compact.sources` for the repositories' Sources.

**Large files (S7).** The Gramps export is not in the repository, so `tests/generated.ts` gains `generatedNotes(count, dialect)`: a 7.0 file of `count` `SNOTE` records and a 5.5.1 file of `NOTE` records continued with `CONC` and `CONT`, each pointed at by a person; the timed Playwright test uses 1478 notes, and a unit test checks that the walk runs once for all counts of a revision.

## Findings of the plan review that no longer apply on this branch

- The evidence section, history (Back and Forward), the card header with the record's name, event cards, `heldAt` on a source and the Events kind are gone with `record-fields-tree`. The first plan's notes about merging them, about the record page's body becoming the tree later, and about a source card's repository section are dropped: the cards are trees from the start, and the repository is a row of the source's tree.
- B3 (the merge) is no longer a merge; the tasks are written against the branch's `drawTree`, `drawRecord` with its `writes` gating, `PageKind` without `event`, and `listRows.ts`/`fileList.ts` without events.

## Spec mechanics

Every MODIFIED block restates the requirement as `record-fields-tree` leaves it once archived, in full, and keeps every scenario name the validator requires. Where a name no longer fits, its text changes and the name stays: *Four kinds* now describes eight buttons; *A record with no row* is a person selected while the Notes are shown; *A record with no card* is the trailer; *A link to a shared note* and *Go to record in the graph* now open cards. `People, families, sources and places` is renamed `The kinds of records the list shows`.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [1478 notes and the deep walk] → The list draws a window, as for people; the walk is one pass, lazy, timed in the Playwright spec on the generated file.
- [Editing a submitter or a custom record] → Limited to what the language service describes, as every tree; the header is excluded by rule.
- [A medium linked only beneath an event] → Counted in the list, not listed on its card; stated.
- [Eight buttons at a sidebar's width] → The measured dropdown.

## Migration Plan

None. A saved list kind unknown to an older build falls back to People, as `readListState` already does; a saved selection `header:` read by an older build names no record and is dropped.

## Open Questions

- The cursor in Source selects a multimedia record today, which the user's wording left out; the plan keeps it.
- Unchanged requirements this plan leaves as `record-fields-tree` leaves them, though they now read narrowly: genealogy-view *A GEDCOM tab is in one of four modes* (List as "people, families or sources"), genealogy-record-detail *Notes, folded where they are long* ("a person, a family or a source"), genealogy-record-card *Portrait and media on a card*, the mention of Back and Forward in *The card lives in the right sidebar*, and *Switching to the source from Marie's birth*. They are `record-fields-tree`'s to settle or a follow-up's.
