## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: What a citation shows
**Reason**: The Evidence section is removed from every card, at the user's request of 2026-11-09 («удали доказательства»): what a record rests on is the lines of its tree, the sources an event cites are rows of its card, and the notes of the vault that link to a record are no longer listed on its card.
**Migration**: The tree of a person, a family or a source shows the citations as lines of the record; the card of an event shows its citations as rows; the source card keeps the list of the records that cite it.
